# ROM 12:1–2 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- **v.1**: Inferential `Therefore` + urgent appeal (`I urge you, brothers, on account of God’s mercy,`) + infinitive exhortation (`to offer your bodies as living sacrifices, holy and pleasing to God,`) + relative definition (`which is your spiritual service of worship.`).
- **v.2**: Negative imperative (`Do not be conformed to this world,`) + contrastive positive imperative (`but be transformed by the renewing of your mind.`) + purpose/result clause (`Then you will be able to test and approve what is the good, pleasing, and perfect will of God.`).

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `living sacrifices` (G2198 *zao* + G2378 *thysia*): ongoing, whole-life self-offering (`산 제물로`).
- `spiritual service of worship` (G3050 *logikos* + G2999 *latreia*): rational, spiritual worship (`영적인 예배`).
- `conformed` (G4964 *syschematizo*): molded externally to fit the pattern of this age (`이 세대를 본받지 말고`).
- `transformed` (G3339 *metamorphoo*): inwardly changed in essential nature (`변화를 받아`).
- `renewing of your mind` (G342 *anakainosis* + G3563 *nous*): renovation of thinking and moral discernment (`마음을 새롭게 함으로`).
