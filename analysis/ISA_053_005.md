# High-Risk Passage Analysis: Isaiah 53:5 (HR014)

- **Reference**: Isaiah 53:5 (`HR014`)
- **Category**: 암송·절기 (Memorization / Passion Week — 고난주간)
- **BSB Base Text**:
  - v.5: "But He was pierced for our transgressions, / He was crushed for our iniquities; / the punishment that brought us peace was upon Him, / and by His stripes we are healed."

## Structural & Key-Term Analysis
1. **Substitutionary Parallelism (Four-Line Climax of the Fourth Servant Song)**:
   - Line 1: "pierced for our transgressions" (`찔린 것은 우리의 허물 때문이요`)
   - Line 2: "crushed for our iniquities" (`상한 것은 우리의 죄악 때문입니다`) — uses glossary term `죄악` for `iniquities`.
   - Line 3: "the punishment that brought us peace was upon Him" (`그가 징벌을 받음으로 우리가 평화를 누리고`)
   - Line 4: "and by His stripes we are healed" (`그가 채찍에 맞음으로 우리가 나음을 입었습니다`)
2. **Middle-School Clarity & Liturgical/Memorization Balance**: Keeps the solemn, well-known Passion Week cadence clear and natural in modern Korean (`~때문입니다`, `~입었습니다`), avoiding archaic endings while preserving theological precision.
