# MAT 5:3–12 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure & Parallelism
- **v.3–10 (Third-person beatitudes)**:
  - Formula: `Blessed [predicate adj.] are [subject: the/those who ...] + for [causal clause: theirs is / they will be ...]`.
  - v.3: `the poor in spirit` → `theirs is the kingdom of heaven`.
  - v.4: `those who mourn` → `will be comforted`.
  - v.5: `the meek` → `will inherit the earth`.
  - v.6: `those who hunger and thirst for righteousness` → `will be filled`.
  - v.7: `the merciful` → `will be shown mercy`.
  - v.8: `the pure in heart` → `will see God`.
  - v.9: `the peacemakers` → `will be called sons of God`.
  - v.10: `those who are persecuted because of righteousness` → `theirs is the kingdom of heaven`.
- **v.11–12 (Second-person shift + imperative)**:
  - v.11: `Blessed are you when [people insult / persecute / falsely say evil against you] because of Me.`
  - v.12: Imperative `Rejoice and be glad` + reason `because great is your reward in heaven, for in the same way they persecuted the prophets before you.`

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `Blessed` (G3107 *makarios*): fortunate, spiritually favored, truly happy (`복이 있다`).
- `poor in spirit`: spiritually humble, recognizing one's need before God (`마음이 겸손하고 가난한 사람들`).
- `meek` (G4239 *praus*): gentle, humble, self-controlled (`온유하고 겸손한 사람들`).
- `righteousness` (G1343 *dikaiosynē*): moral uprightness / right standing (`의로움`).
