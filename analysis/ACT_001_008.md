# ACT 1:8 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- Contrastive conjunction `But` + future promise (`you will receive power when the Holy Spirit comes upon you,`) + sequential commission (`and you will be My witnesses`) + fourfold geographical progression (`in Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the ends of the earth.`).

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `power` (G1411 *dynamis*): spiritual strength, ability (`능력`).
- `comes upon you` (*epelthontos ... eph' hymas*): descends on you (`너희에게 임하시면`).
- `witnesses` (G3144 *martys*): those who testify to what they have seen and heard (`증인`).
- `to the ends of the earth`: to the farthest parts of the world (`땅 끝까지`).
