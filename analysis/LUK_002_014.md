# LUK 2:14 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- Two parallel acclamation clauses in poetic couplet form:
  1. `Glory to God in the highest,` (dative of recipient `to God` + locative/superlative `in the highest [heavens]`)
  2. `and on earth peace to men on whom His favor rests!` (locative contrast `on earth` + `peace` + dative `to men` + relative clause `on whom His favor rests`, reflecting *anthrōpois eudokias* — people of God's good pleasure/favor).

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `Glory` (G1391 *doxa*): honor, praise, radiance (`영광`).
- `in the highest` (G5310 *hypsistos*): in the highest heaven (`지극히 높은 곳`).
- `peace` (G1515 *eirēnē*): wholeness, reconciliation, peace (`평화`).
- `on whom His favor rests` (G2107 *eudokia*): people whom God favors / delights in (`하나님이 기뻐하시는 사람들`).
