# High-Risk Passage Analysis: Psalm 23:1–6 (HR007)

- **Reference**: Psalm 23:1–6 (`HR007`)
- **Category**: 암송·장례 (Memorization & Funeral)
- **BSB Base Text**:
  - v.1: "A Psalm of David. / The LORD is my shepherd; / I shall not want."
  - v.2: "He makes me lie down in green pastures; / He leads me beside quiet waters."
  - v.3: "He restores my soul; / He guides me in the paths of righteousness / for the sake of His name."
  - v.4: "Even though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, / I will fear no evil, / for You are with me; / Your rod and Your staff, they comfort me."
  - v.5: "You prepare a table before me / in the presence of my enemies. / You anoint my head with oil; / my cup overflows."
  - v.6: "Surely goodness and mercy will follow me / all the days of my life, / and I will dwell in the house of the LORD / forever."

## Structural & Poetic Analysis
1. **Pastoral & Host Metaphors**: Psalm 23 shifts from the **Shepherd** metaphor (vv. 1–4) to the **Gracious Host** metaphor (vv. 5–6). Preserving this warm, personal, and reassuring cadence is vital for both memorization and funeral settings.
2. **Direct & Middle-School-Accessible Clarity**:
   - "I shall not want" → `내게 부족함이 없다` (clear and natural without archaic phrasing).
   - "valley of the shadow of death" → `죽음의 그림자가 드리운 골짜기` (preserves the imagery clearly, with BSB footnote noting "또는 '짙은 어둠의 골짜기'").
   - "Your rod and Your staff, they comfort me" → `주의 막대기와 주의 지팡이가 나를 위로합니다`.
   - "goodness and mercy" → `선하심과 자비` (or `선하심과 인자하심`; `선하심과 자비` is natural and accessible).
3. **Glossary Alignment**:
   - `LORD` → `주` (or `주님` in direct address where natural; using `주` in vv. 1, 6 keeps strict glossary consistency).
   - `paths of righteousness` → `의로움의 길` (matches `righteousness` = `의로움`).
   - Footnotes in v. 4 (`또는 "짙은 어둠의 골짜기"`) and v. 6 (`직역하면 "긴 날들 동안"`) are preserved faithfully.
