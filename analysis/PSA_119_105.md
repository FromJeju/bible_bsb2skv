# High-Risk Passage Analysis: Psalm 119:105 (HR009)

- **Reference**: Psalm 119:105 (`HR009`)
- **Category**: 암송 (Memorization)
- **BSB Base Text**:
  - v.105: "Your word is a lamp to my feet / and a light to my path."

## Structural & Key-Term Analysis
1. **Acrostic Placement**: Verse 105 opens the *Nun* (`נ`) stanza (vv. 105–112) of the 22-stanza acrostic Psalm 119, where every stanza meditates on God's Torah/word.
2. **Synonymous Parallelism**:
   - "Your word" (`주의 말씀`)
   - "a lamp to my feet" (`제 발에 등불이요`)
   - "a light to my path" (`제 길에 빛입니다`)
3. **Middle-School Clarity & Memorization Cadence**: Keeping the concise, rhythmic two-line parallelism (`주의 말씀은 제 발에 등불이요 / 제 길에 빛입니다`) ensures immediate comprehension and smooth memorization without archaic endings.
