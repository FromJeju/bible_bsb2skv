# High-Risk Passage Analysis: Isaiah 41:10 (HR013)

- **Reference**: Isaiah 41:10 (`HR013`)
- **Category**: 암송 (Memorization)
- **BSB Base Text**:
  - v.10: "Do not fear, for I am with you; / do not be afraid, for I am your God. / I will strengthen you; I will surely help you; / I will uphold you with My righteous right hand."

## Structural & Key-Term Analysis
1. **Parallel Reassurance & Threefold Promise**:
   - Reassurance 1: "Do not fear, for I am with you" (`두려워하지 마라. 내가 너와 함께 있다.`)
   - Reassurance 2: "do not be afraid, for I am your God" (`놀라지 마라. 나는 네 하나님이다.`)
   - Threefold Promise: strengthen (`내가 너를 강하게 하고`), help (`반드시 너를 도우며`), uphold with righteous right hand (`내 의로운 오른손으로 너를 붙들어 주겠다`).
2. **Middle-School Clarity**: Replaces archaic endings (`~하리라`, `~함이라`) with direct, clear Korean (`함께 있다`, `하나님이다`, `붙들어 주겠다`) while maintaining the memorizable cadence.
