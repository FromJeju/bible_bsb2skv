# GEN 1:1 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- Opening temporal phrase + transitive clause with compound object:
  1. Temporal setting: `In the beginning` (absolute start of created time and cosmos).
  2. Subject + verb: `God created` (divine creative act, H1254 *bara*, uniquely used of God).
  3. Merism object: `the heavens and the earth.` (the entire ordered universe).

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `In the beginning` (H7225 *reshit*): at the very beginning (`태초에`).
- `God` (H430 *Elohim*): the one true God (`하나님께서`).
- `created` (H1254 *bara*): brought into existence (`창조하셨다`).
- `the heavens and the earth`: sky/heavenly realm and earth, i.e., the whole universe (`하늘과 땅을`).
