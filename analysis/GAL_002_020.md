# GAL 2:20 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- **Clause 1 (Past-Present State)**: `I have been crucified with Christ, and I no longer live, but Christ lives in me.` — passive perfect (`have been crucified`) + negation of autonomous self (`no longer live`) + indwelling Christ (`Christ lives in me`).
- **Clause 2 (Present Life by Faith)**: `The life I live in the body, I live by faith in the Son of God, who loved me and gave Himself up for me.` — relative clarification of embodied life + instrumental `by faith` + two aorist/past participles describing Christ's self-giving love.

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `crucified with Christ` (G4957 *systauroo*): co-crucified, sharing in Christ's death (`그리스도와 함께 십자가에 못 박혔습니다`).
- `Christ lives in me`: ongoing indwelling life of Christ (`내 안에 그리스도께서 사십니다`).
- `by faith in the Son of God`: trusting reliance on the Son of God (`하나님의 아들을 믿는 믿음 안에서`).
- `gave Himself up for me`: voluntary substitutionary self-sacrifice (`나를 위해 자기 자신을 내주신`).
