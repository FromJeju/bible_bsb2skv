# REV 3:20 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- Attention-calling interjection (`Behold,`) + present-tense posture + conditional invitation with reciprocal promise:
  1. Present posture: `I stand at the door and knock.` (continuous patient waiting at the threshold).
  2. Condition: `If anyone hears My voice and opens the door,` (individual responsiveness to Christ's call).
  3. Reciprocal promise: `I will come in and dine with him, and he with Me.` (intimate table fellowship).

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `Behold` (G2400 *idou*): look, pay attention (`보아라`).
- `stand ... and knock` (G2476 *histēmi* + G2925 *krouō*): standing persistently and knocking (`문 밖에 서서 문을 두드리고 있다`).
- `hears My voice` (G191 *akouō*): listens and responds (`내 음성을 듣고`).
- `dine` (G1172 *deipneō*): share the main evening meal, symbolizing close communion (`함께 먹고`).
