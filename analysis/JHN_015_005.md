# JHN 15:5 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- Metaphorical identification (`I am the vine and you are the branches.`) + participial condition & promise (`The one who remains in Me, and I in him, will bear much fruit.`) + causal explanation (`For apart from Me you can do nothing.`).

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `vine` (G288 *ampelos*) / `branches` (G2814 *klēma*): grapevine and its branches (`포도나무` / `가지`).
- `remains` (G3306 *menō*): abides, stays connected (`머물러 있다`).
- `bear much fruit`: produce abundant spiritual fruit (`많은 열매를 맺는다`).
- `apart from Me` (*chōris emou*): separated from Me (`나를 떠나서는`).
