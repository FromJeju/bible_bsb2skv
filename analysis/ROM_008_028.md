# ROM 8:28 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- Assurance formula `And we know that` + main clause (`God works all things together for the good`) + dative/benefactive object (`of those who love Him,`) + appositional participial clause (`who are called according to His purpose.`).

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `God works ... together` (G4903 *synergeo*): cooperates, causes all things to work together (`하나님께서 모든 것을 합력하여`).
- `for the good` (G18 *agathos*): for ultimate spiritual good/benefit (`선을 이루신다는 것을`).
- `those who love Him`: believers characterized by love for God (`하나님을 사랑하는 사람들`).
- `called according to His purpose` (G2822 *kletos* + G4286 *prothesis*): effectually called in line with God's sovereign plan (`그분의 뜻대로 부르심을 받은 사람들을 위해`).
