# Deuteronomy 6:4–5 (HR004) — The Shema

## Source Text (BSB)
- **v. 4**: "Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God, the LORD is One."
  - Footnote: "Or *The LORD our God is One LORD* or *The LORD is our God, the LORD is One* or *The LORD is our God, the LORD alone*; cited in Mark 12:29"
- **v. 5**: "And you shall love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your strength."
  - Footnote: "Cited in Matthew 22:37, Mark 12:30, and Luke 10:27"

## Structural & Theological Analysis
- **v. 4 (Shema)**: The foundational confession of Israel's monotheism and covenant loyalty.
  - `Hear, O Israel`: `이스라엘아, 들으십시오.` — In Deuteronomy 5–26, Moses addresses the congregation in polite/formal exhortative Korean (`~십시오` / `~습니다`), matching the speech framing in chapter 1–5.
  - `The LORD our God, the LORD is One`: `주 우리 하나님은 오직 한 분이신 주이십니다.` — Preserves both occurrences of `LORD` (`주`, glossary-enforced) and `God` (`하나님`) while expressing the oneness/uniqueness of `주` clearly for middle-school readers. Alternative readings from the BSB footnote are preserved in the Korean footnote.
- **v. 5 (Love Commandment)**:
  - `with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your strength`: `온 마음과 온 영혼과 온 힘을 다하여` — Preserves the three-fold totality (`heart`, `soul`, `strength`) in natural, memorable Korean cadence suitable for recitation (`암송`), avoiding archaic `성품` or `뜻`.
  - `love the LORD your God`: `주 여러분의 하나님을 사랑하십시오.` — Clear active imperative/exhortation matching Moses' direct address to Israel.
