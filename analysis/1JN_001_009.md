# 1JN 1:9 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- Conditional promise (`If ...`, 3rd-class condition) with two paired divine attributes and two paired infinitives of result:
  1. Condition: `If we confess our sins,` (ongoing/genuine acknowledgment of sin).
  2. Divine character: `He is faithful and just` (faithful to His covenant promise, just because of Christ's atonement).
  3. Dual result: `to forgive us our sins` + `and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.`

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `confess` (G3670 *homologeō*): acknowledge openly, agree with God about sin (`자백하면`).
- `faithful` (G4103 *pistos*): trustworthy, keeping His word (`신실하시고`).
- `just` (G1342 *dikaios*): righteous, fair (`의로워서`).
- `forgive` (G863 *aphiēmi*): release, pardon (`용서해 주시고`).
- `cleanse` (G2511 *katharizō*): purify completely (`깨끗하게 해 주실 것입니다`).
- `all unrighteousness` (G93 *adikia*): every wrongdoing and moral stain (`모든 불의에서`).
