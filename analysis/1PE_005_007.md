# 1PE 5:7 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- Imperative main clause followed by a causal subordinate clause:
  1. Main command: `Cast all your anxiety on Him,` (aorist participle in Greek functioning imperatively alongside v.6 "Humble yourselves", meaning a decisive transfer of every worry).
  2. Causal reason: `because He cares for you.` (ongoing personal care and concern).

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `Cast` (G1977 *epiriptō*): throw upon, unload completely onto someone (`다 맡기십시오`).
- `all your anxiety` (G3308 *merimna*): every anxious care, worry, or distraction (`여러분의 모든 염려를`).
- `on Him`: on God, whose mighty hand was just mentioned in v.6 (`그분께`).
- `cares for you` (G3199 *melō*): it matters to Him; He actively looks after you (`그분이 여러분을 돌보시기 때문입니다`).
