# EPH 2:8–9 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- **v.8**: Causal `For` + periphrastic perfect passive (`it is by grace you have been saved through faith,`) + negative-positive clarification (`and this not from yourselves; it is the gift of God,`).
- **v.9**: Negative source exclusion (`not by works,`) + purpose clause (`so that no one can boast.`).

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `by grace` (G5485 *charis*): unmerited divine favor, ultimate basis of salvation (`은혜로`).
- `you have been saved` (G4982 *sozo*): past completed salvation with ongoing present result (`구원을 받았습니다`).
- `through faith` (G1223 *dia* + G4102 *pistis*): instrumental means (`믿음을 통해`).
- `gift of God` (G1435 *doron*): free divine present, not earned (`하나님의 선물입니다`).
- `not by works` (G2041 *ergon*): excluded human achievement/merit (`행위에서 난 것이 아니니`).
