# MAT 11:28 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- **Imperative invitation**: `Come to Me`
- **Vocative / Addressee**: `all you who are weary and burdened` (two coordinated adjectives describing people exhausted by heavy labor/loads)
- **Promise (Result clause)**: `and I will give you rest.`

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `weary` (G2872 *kopiaō*): exhausted from hard labor (`지치고 지친 / 수고로 지친`).
- `burdened` (G5412 *phortizō*): weighed down with heavy loads (`무거운 짐을 진`).
- `rest` (G373 *anapauō*): refreshment, relief, rest (`쉼 / 편히 쉼`).
