# MAT 6:9–13 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- **v.9a (Introductory frame)**: `So then, this is how you should pray:`
- **v.9b (Address + 1st petition)**: Vocative `Our Father in heaven` + passive imperative/jussive `hallowed be Your name` (may Your name be treated as holy).
- **v.10 (2nd & 3rd petitions)**: `Your kingdom come`, `Your will be done` + locative comparison `on earth as it is in heaven`.
- **v.11 (4th petition)**: Imperative `Give us this day our daily bread.`
- **v.12 (5th petition)**: `And forgive us our debts` + comparison `as we also have forgiven our debtors.` (Note: BSB uses *debts/debtors* literally.)
- **v.13 (6th petition)**: Negative imperative `And lead us not into temptation`, adversative `but deliver us from the evil one.`

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `hallowed` (G37 *hagiazō*): treated as holy, honored as sacred (`거룩하게 여김을 받으소서 / 거룩히 여겨지게 하소서`).
- `debts` / `debtors` (G3783 *opheilēma* / G3781 *opheiletēs*): moral obligations/sins owed to God and others (`빚 / 잘못`).
- `the evil one` (G4190 *ponēros*): personal masculine singular in BSB (`악한 자`).
