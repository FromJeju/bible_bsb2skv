# Joshua 1:9 (HR005) — Be Strong and Courageous

## Source Text (BSB)
- **v. 9**: "Have I not commanded you to be strong and courageous? Do not be afraid; do not be discouraged, for the LORD your God is with you wherever you go."

## Structural & Key-Term Analysis
- **Rhetorical question opener**: `Have I not commanded you to be strong and courageous?` — Reinforces the repeated charge from vv. 6–7 (`be strong and courageous`), framed as an emphatic reminder (`내가 너에게 강하고 담대하라고 명령하지 않았느냐?`).
- **Parallel negative commands**: `Do not be afraid; do not be discouraged` — `두려워하지 말고, 낙심하지 말아라.` Simple, direct middle-schooler vocabulary (`두려워하다`, `낙심하다`), avoiding stiff Sino-Korean `놀라지 말라`.
- **Covenantal promise**: `for the LORD your God is with you wherever you go` — `네가 어디로 가든지 주 네 하나님이 너와 함께하기 때문이다.` Preserves `LORD` → `주` and `God` → `하나님` per glossary, and uses plain imperative/declarative register (`~아라`/`~다`) for God's direct speech to Joshua.
