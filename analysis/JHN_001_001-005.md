# JHN 1:1–5 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- **v.1**: Three coordinated clauses establishing the pre-existence, relationship, and deity of the Word: `In the beginning was the Word` + `and the Word was with God` + `and the Word was God.`
- **v.2**: Summary echo: `He was with God in the beginning.`
- **v.3**: Creation agency (positive + emphatic negative): `Through Him all things were made, and without Him nothing was made that has been made.`
- **v.4**: Source of life and light: `In Him was life, and that life was the light of men.`
- **v.5**: Present-tense conflict/triumph: `The Light shines in the darkness, and the darkness has not overcome it.`

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `Word` (G3056 *logos*): the divine Word (`말씀`).
- `with God` (G4314 *pros ton theon*): in personal fellowship with God (`하나님과 함께`).
- `was God` (G2316 *theos*): qualitative/predicative assertion of deity (`곧 하나님이셨다`).
- `overcome` (G2638 *katalambanō*): overpower, extinguish, or comprehend (`이기지 못했다`, footnote: `또는 '깨닫지 못했다'`).
