# 2CO 5:17 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- Inferential `Therefore` + conditional clause (`if anyone is in Christ,`) + verbless nominal declaration (`he is a new creation.`) + two short declarative results (`The old has passed away. Behold, the new has come!`).

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `in Christ` (G1722 *en* + G5547 *Christos*): union with Christ (`그리스도 안에 있으면`).
- `a new creation` (G2537 *kainos* + G2937 *ktisis*): qualitatively new creation/creature brought into being by God (`새로운 피조물입니다`).
- `The old has passed away` (G744 *archaios* + G3928 *parerchomai*): the former way of existence is gone (`옛것은 지나갔습니다`).
- `the new has come!` (G2537 *kainos* + G1096 *ginomai*): the new order has arrived (`새것이 되었습니다!`).
