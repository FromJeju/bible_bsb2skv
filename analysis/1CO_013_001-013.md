# 1CO 13:1–13 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- **vv.1–3 (Indispensability of Love)**: Three conditional sentences (`If I ... but have not love, ...`) escalating from tongues (v.1) to prophecy/faith (v.2) to extreme generosity/self-sacrifice (v.3), each ending in zero spiritual value without love.
- **vv.4–7 (Character of Love)**: Personification of `love` (G26 *agape*) via 15 verbs/clauses: two positive (`is patient`, `is kind`), eight negative (`does not envy`, `boast`, `proud`, `rude`, `self-seeking`, `easily angered`, `keeps no account of wrongs`, `takes no pleasure in evil`), one contrastive (`rejoices in the truth`), and four universal active verbs (`bears`, `believes`, `hopes`, `endures all things`).
- **vv.8–12 (Permanence of Love)**: Contrast between eternal `love` (`never fails`) and temporary partial gifts (`prophecies`, `tongues`, `knowledge`), illustrated by childhood vs. maturity (v.11) and dim mirror vs. face-to-face sight (v.12).
- **v.13 (Climax)**: Concluding triad (`faith, hope, and love`), elevating `love` as `the greatest of these`.

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `love` (G26 *agape*): self-giving, covenantal love (`사랑`).
- `never fails` (G1601 *ekpipto* / *pipto*): never falls, never ends or loses effectiveness (`사랑은 결코 사라지지 않습니다`).
- `the perfect` (G5046 *teleios*): completeness, maturity, the eschatological fullness (`온전한 것이`).
- `dim reflection as in a mirror` (G135 *esoptron* + G135 *ainigma*): indirect, unclear view (`거울로 보듯 희미하게`).
