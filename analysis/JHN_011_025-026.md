# JHN 11:25–26 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- **v.25**: Self-identification predicate (`I am the resurrection and the life.`) + universal conditional promise (`Whoever believes in Me will live, even though he dies.`).
- **v.26**: Parallel universal promise (`And everyone who lives and believes in Me will never die.`) + direct personal question (`Do you believe this?`).

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `resurrection` (G386 *anastasis*): rising from the dead (`부활`).
- `life` (G2222 *zōē*): true, eternal life (`생명`).
- `even though he dies` (*kan apothanē*): even if he physically dies (`설령 죽더라도`).
- `will never die` (*ou mē apothanē eis ton aiōna*): emphatic double negative — will never die eternally (`영원히 죽지 않을 것이다`).
