# 2TI 3:16 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- Universal subject `All Scripture` + verbless/copula predicate with compound adjectives (`is God-breathed and is useful`) + fourfold `for`-phrase sequence indicating purpose/application:
  1. `for instruction` (teaching truth)
  2. `for conviction` (reproof/exposing error)
  3. `for correction` (restoring to uprightness)
  4. `for training in righteousness` (disciplined moral formation).

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `All Scripture` (G3956 *pas* + G1124 *graphe*): every sacred Scripture (`모든 성경은`).
- `God-breathed` (G2315 *theopneustos*): inspired/breathed out by God (`하나님의 숨결이 불어넣어진 것으로`).
- `instruction` (G1319 *didaskalia*): teaching/doctrine (`가르침과`).
- `conviction` (G1650 *elegmos*): reproof/rebuke (`책망과`).
- `correction` (G1882 *epanorthosis*): setting straight (`바로잡음과`).
- `training in righteousness` (G3809 *paideia* + G1343 *dikaiosyne*): moral/spiritual training (`의로움으로 훈련하기에`).
