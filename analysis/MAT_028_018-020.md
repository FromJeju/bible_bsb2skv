# MAT 28:18–20 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- **v.18**: Narrative frame `Then Jesus came to them and said` + declarative passive `All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to Me.`
- **v.19**: Inferential conjunction `Therefore` + main imperative `go and make disciples of all nations` + two instrumental/manner participles:
  1) `baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit`
  2) **v.20a**: `and teaching them to obey all that I have commanded you.`
- **v.20b**: Concluding promise `And surely I am with you always, even to the end of the age.`

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `authority` (G1849 *exousia*): full power and right to rule (`모든 권한 / 권세`).
- `make disciples` (G3100 *mathēteuō*): bring people to become followers/learners of Jesus (`제자로 삼아라`).
- `to obey` (G5083 *tēreō*): keep, observe, put into practice (`지켜 행하도록 / 순종하도록`).
- `the end of the age` (G4930 *synteleia* + G165 *aiōn*): the close of this present age (`세상 끝날까지`).
