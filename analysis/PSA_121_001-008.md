# High-Risk Passage Analysis: Psalm 121:1–8 (HR010)

- **Reference**: Psalm 121:1–8 (`HR010`)
- **Category**: 암송 (Memorization)
- **BSB Base Text**:
  - v.1: "A song of ascents. / I lift up my eyes to the hills. / From where does my help come?"
  - v.2: "My help comes from the LORD, / the Maker of heaven and earth."
  - v.3: "He will not allow your foot to slip; / your Protector will not slumber."
  - v.4: "Behold, the Protector of Israel / will neither slumber nor sleep."
  - v.5: "The LORD is your keeper; / the LORD is the shade on your right hand."
  - v.6: "The sun will not strike you by day, / nor the moon by night."
  - v.7: "The LORD will guard you from all evil; / He will preserve your soul."
  - v.8: "The LORD will watch over your coming and going, / both now and forevermore."

## Structural & Key-Term Analysis
1. **Pilgrim Song of Ascents**: Opens with a first-person question and confession (vv. 1–2) before shifting to second-person assurance of protection (vv. 3–8).
2. **Repeated Guarding Motif (*shamar*)**: BSB renders the Hebrew root with varied synonyms—"Protector" (vv. 3–4), "keeper" (v. 5), "guard" (v. 7), "preserve" (v. 7), and "watch over" (v. 8). Using consistent, natural Korean equivalents (`지키시는 분`, `지켜 주시며`, `보전하실`, `지켜보실`) preserves the thematic unity for memorization.
3. **Middle-School Clarity**: Natural polite prose-poetry endings (`~합니다`, `~십니다`, `~것입니다`) keep this well-known memorization psalm clear, rhythmic, and accessible without archaic church diction.
