# JER 29:11 Structural & Key-Term Analysis (HR015)

- **Reference**: Jeremiah 29:11 (`HR015`, Category: Memorization / `암송`)
- **BSB Text**:
  > "For I know the plans I have for you, declares the LORD, plans to prosper you and not to harm you, to give you a future and a hope."

## 1. Grammatical & Structural Breakdown
1. **Causal Opening (`For I know...`)**: Grounded in v. 10 (the 70-year Babylonian exile promise). The divine speaker (`declares the LORD`) personally guarantees that the exile is not abandonment.
2. **Emphatic First-Person Knowledge (`I know the plans I have for you`)**: Highlights intentionality and sovereign foresight (`내가 너희를 위해 품고 있는 계획들을 내가 잘 알고 있다`).
3. **Antithetical Purpose Pair (`plans to prosper you and not to harm you`)**: Contrasts welfare/shalom (`prosper` -> `평안하게 하려는`) with disaster/evil (`harm` -> `해치려는`).
4. **Teleological Infinitive (`to give you a future and a hope`)**: Pairs two forward-looking nouns (`미래와 희망을 주려는`).

## 2. Key Terms & SKV Rendering Decisions
- `the LORD`: Rendered as `주` per `glossary.csv`.
- `plans to prosper you`: Rendered as `너희를 평안하게 하려는 계획` (capturing Hebrew *shalom* / BSB *prosper* naturally for middle-school readers without sounding strictly materialistic).
- `and not to harm you`: Rendered as `너희를 해치려는 것이 아니라` for clear contrast.
- `a future and a hope`: Rendered as `미래와 희망` — direct, natural, and memorable.

## 3. Final SKV Translation
> “주께서 말씀하신다. 내가 너희를 위해 품고 있는 계획들을 내가 잘 알고 있다. 그것은 너희를 해치려는 것이 아니라 평안하게 하려는 계획이며, 너희에게 미래와 희망을 주려는 것이다.”
