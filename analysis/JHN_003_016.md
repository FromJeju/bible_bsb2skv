# JHN 3:16 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- Causal conjunction `For` + main clause of degree/action `God so loved the world that He gave His one and only Son,` + purpose/result clause `that everyone who believes in Him shall not perish but have eternal life.`

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `so loved` (G3779 *houtōs* + G25 *agapaō*): loved in this way / to such a degree (`이처럼 사랑하셔서`).
- `one and only Son` (G3439 *monogenēs* + G5207 *huios*): unique, one-of-a-kind Son (`외아들`).
- `everyone who believes in Him` (G3956 *pas* + G4100 *pisteuō*): anyone who trusts in Him (`그분을 믿는 사람은 누구든지`).
- `perish` / `have eternal life`: contrast between destruction/ruin and everlasting life with God (`멸망하지 않고 영원한 생명을 얻게 하셨다`).
