# High-Risk Passage Analysis: Psalm 46:10 (HR008)

- **Reference**: Psalm 46:10 (`HR008`)
- **Category**: 암송 (Memorization)
- **BSB Base Text**:
  - v.10: "“Be still and know that I am God; / I will be exalted among the nations, / I will be exalted over the earth.”"

## Structural & Key-Term Analysis
1. **Divine Oracle within the Psalm**: After describing cosmic upheaval (vv. 1–3), the secure city of God (vv. 4–7), and the LORD's cessation of war (vv. 8–9), v. 10 introduces God's direct first-person voice speaking to both the warring nations and His people.
2. **"Be still" (`가만히 멈추어` / `잠잠히 있어`)**: The Hebrew verb *raphah* means "let go," "cease striving," or "stop fighting/fretting." Translating it as `잠잠히 멈추어` (or `가만히 멈추어`) clearly conveys ceasing anxious striving and recognizing God's sovereignty, natural for middle-school readers and memorization.
3. **"know that I am God" (`내가 하나님임을 알아라`)**: Uses the glossary standard `God` → `하나님` and clear imperative phrasing.
4. **Parallel Exaltation Climax**: "I will be exalted among the nations, / I will be exalted over the earth" → `내가 민족들 가운데서 높임을 받고 / 온 땅 위에서 높임을 받을 것이다`.
