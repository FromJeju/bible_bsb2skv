# EXO 20:1-17 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- Covenant preamble + 10 apodictic commandments (`You shall...` / `You shall not...`):
  1. **Preamble (vv.1–2)**: Historical grace precedes law — `I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slavery.`
  2. **Vertical Commandments (vv.3–11, 1–4)**: Exclusivity of worship (v.3), prohibition of idols with generational warning/mercy (vv.4–6), reverence for God's name (v.7), Sabbath rest grounded in creation (vv.8–11).
  3. **Horizontal Commandments (vv.12–17, 5–10)**: Honor parents with promise (v.12), prohibitions against murder (v.13), adultery (v.14), theft (v.15), false witness (v.16), and coveting (v.17).

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `house of slavery`: Egypt as bondage (`종살이하던 집`).
- `jealous God` (H7067 *qanna*): zealously protective of exclusive covenant loyalty (`질투하는 하나님`).
- `loving devotion` (H2617 *chesed*): steadfast covenant love (`한결같은 사랑`).
- `in vain` (H7723 *shav*): emptiness, falsehood, misuse (`함부로`).
- `Sabbath` (H7676 *shabbat*): day of sacred rest (`안식일`).
- `covet` (H2530 *chamad*): desire improperly to take what belongs to another (`탐내지 마라`).
