# High-Risk Passage Analysis: Proverbs 3:5–6 (HR011)

- **Reference**: Proverbs 3:5–6 (`HR011`)
- **Category**: 암송 (Memorization)
- **BSB Base Text**:
  - v.5: "Trust in the LORD with all your heart, / and lean not on your own understanding;"
  - v.6: "in all your ways acknowledge Him, / and He will make your paths straight."

## Structural & Key-Term Analysis
1. **Parallel Imperative + Promise Structure**:
   - Positive command: "Trust in the LORD with all your heart" (`너의 온 마음을 다하여 주를 의지하고`)
   - Negative contrast: "and lean not on your own understanding" (`너 자신의 명철을 의지하지 마라`)
   - Comprehensive scope: "in all your ways acknowledge Him" (`너의 모든 길에서 주를 인정하여라`)
   - Resulting promise: "and He will make your paths straight" (`그러면 주께서 너의 길을 곧게 하실 것이다`)
2. **Middle-School Clarity & Memorization Cadence**: Avoiding archaic forms (`내 명철을 의지하지 말지어다`, `지도하시리라`) while keeping clear, rhythmic two-line couplets makes this key memorization passage immediately natural for middle-school readers.
