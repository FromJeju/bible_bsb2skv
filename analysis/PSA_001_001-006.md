# High-Risk Passage Analysis: Psalm 1:1–6 (HR006)

- **Reference**: Psalm 1:1–6 (`HR006`)
- **Category**: 암송 (Memorization)
- **BSB Base Text**:
  - v.1: "Blessed is the man / who does not walk in the counsel of the wicked, / or set foot on the path of sinners, / or sit in the seat of mockers."
  - v.2: "But his delight is in the Law of the LORD, / and on His law he meditates day and night."
  - v.3: "He is like a tree planted by streams of water, / yielding its fruit in season, / whose leaf does not wither, / and who prospers in all he does."
  - v.4: "Not so the wicked! / For they are like chaff driven off by the wind."
  - v.5: "Therefore the wicked will not stand in the judgment, / nor sinners in the assembly of the righteous."
  - v.6: "For the LORD guards the path of the righteous, / but the way of the wicked will perish."

## Structural & Poetic Analysis
1. **Two-Way Wisdom Contrast**: Psalm 1 opens Book I of the Psalter by contrasting the path of the righteous (vv. 1–3) and the fate of the wicked (vv. 4–5), culminating in a summary theological statement (v. 6).
2. **Triadic Progression in v. 1**: The verbs "walk" (`걷다`), "set foot / stand" (`서다`), and "sit" (`앉다`) trace an escalating progression of alignment with "the wicked" (`악인들`), "sinners" (`죄인들`), and "mockers" (`비웃는 자들`). Preserving this clear spatial/behavioral progression is essential for memorization and clarity.
3. **Torah Piety in v. 2**: "Law of the LORD" (`주의 율법`) and "meditates" (`묵상한다` / `되새긴다` — rendered clearly as `되새긴다` or `깊이 생각한다`) express active, continual reflection rather than passive reading.
4. **Botanical vs. Agricultural Similes (vv. 3–4)**: A well-watered, fruit-bearing tree (`시냇가에 심겨진 나무`) stands in sharp contrast to wind-blown chaff (`바람에 날려 가는 겨`).
5. **Judgment & Final Verdict (vv. 5–6)**: "Stand in the judgment" (`심판 날에 버티고 서다`) and "guards the path of the righteous" (`의로운 자들의 길을 지켜 주신다`) use glossary-aligned terms (`주`, `의로운 자들`).

## Key Glossary & Style Decisions
- `LORD` → `주`
- `righteous` → `의로운 자들`
- `Law of the LORD` → `주의 율법`
- `Blessed is the man` → `복이 있는 사람` (clear, natural Korean cadence suitable for middle-school readers and memorization).
