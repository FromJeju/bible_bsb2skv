# High-Risk Passage Analysis: Isaiah 40:31 (HR012)

- **Reference**: Isaiah 40:31 (`HR012`)
- **Category**: 암송 (Memorization)
- **BSB Base Text**:
  - v.31: "But those who wait upon the LORD will renew their strength; / they will mount up with wings like eagles; / they will run and not grow weary, / they will walk and not faint."

## Structural & Key-Term Analysis
1. **Four-Line Climactic Promise**:
   - Condition & renewal: "those who wait upon the LORD will renew their strength" (`오직 주를 기다리는 사람들은 새 힘을 얻을 것이니`)
   - Three-fold endurance imagery: mounting up like eagles (`독수리처럼 날개를 펴고 올라갈 것이요`), running without growing weary (`달려가도 지치지 않고`), walking without fainting (`걸어가도 피곤하지 않을 것입니다`).
2. **Middle-School Clarity & Memorization Cadence**: Clear, natural Korean phrasing (`새 힘을 얻을 것이니`, `지치지 않고`, `피곤하지 않을 것입니다`) preserves the memorable rhythm of the verse while avoiding archaic endings (`~하리로다`, `~할지어다`).
