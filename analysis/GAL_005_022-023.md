# GAL 5:22–23 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- Contrastive `But` + singular collective noun (`the fruit of the Spirit is`) + nine-fold polysyndetic/asyndetic list of virtues:
  - `love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control.`
- Concluding legal maxim: `Against such things there is no law.`

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `fruit of the Spirit` (G2590 *karpos* + G4151 *pneuma*): singular organic outcome produced by the Holy Spirit (`성령의 열매는`).
- Nine virtues: `love` (`사랑`), `joy` (`기쁨`), `peace` (`평화`), `patience` (`오래 참음`), `kindness` (`친절`), `goodness` (`선함`), `faithfulness` (`신실함`), `gentleness` (`온유`), `self-control` (`절제`).
- `Against such things there is no law`: no Mosaic or moral law forbids or condemns these virtues (`이런 것들을 금지하는 법은 없습니다`).
