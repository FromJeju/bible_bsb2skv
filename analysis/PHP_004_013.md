# PHP 4:13 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- Simple declarative claim of empowerment: `I can do all things` (contextualized by vv.11–12: contentment in both abundance and need) + instrumental prepositional phrase (`through Christ who gives me strength.`).

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `I can do all things` (G2480 *ischyo* + G3956 *pas*): I have strength/ability for all circumstances (`나는 모든 것을 할 수 있습니다`).
- `through Christ who gives me strength` (G1722 *en* + G1743 *endynamoo*): empowered in union with Christ (`내게 능력을 주시는 그리스도 안에서`).
- Textual note: `NA, SBL, NE, WH` read "the One who gives me strength", while `BYZ, TR` explicitly read "Christ".
