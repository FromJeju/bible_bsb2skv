# PHP 4:6–7 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- **v.6**: Negative imperative (`Be anxious for nothing,`) + contrastive positive imperative (`but in everything, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God.`).
- **v.7**: Sequential promise (`And the peace of God,`) + parenthetical relative clause (`which surpasses all understanding,`) + future result (`will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus.`).

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `Be anxious for nothing` (G3309 *merimnao*): do not be consumed by worry (`아무것도 염려하지 말고`).
- `prayer and petition, with thanksgiving` (G4335 *proseuche*, G1162 *deesis*, G2169 *eucharistia*): general prayer, specific supplication, and gratefulness (`기도와 간구로, 감사함으로`).
- `surpasses all understanding` (G5242 *hyperecho* + G3563 *nous*): transcends human comprehension (`모든 이해를 뛰어넘는`).
- `will guard` (G5432 *phroureo*): military metaphor of standing watch/garrisoning (`지켜 주실 것입니다`).
