# REV 21:4 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- Poetic/prophetic promise quoting Isaiah 25:8 followed by four negated nouns and a causal clause:
  1. Positive divine action: `‘He will wipe away every tear from their eyes,’`
  2. Fourfold negation: `and there will be no more death / or mourning or crying or pain,`
  3. Causal ground: `for the former things have passed away.”`

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `wipe away` (G1813 *exaleiphō*): blot out, wipe completely dry (`닦아 주실 것이다`).
- `every tear` (G1144 *dakryon*): every single tear of sorrow (`모든 눈물을`).
- `no more death or mourning or crying or pain`: total removal of mortality and grief (`죽음도 없고, 슬픔도 울부짖음도 아픔도 다시는 없을 것이다`).
- `the former things` (G4413 *prōtos*): the old fallen order (`이전 것들이`).
- `have passed away` (G565 *aperchomai*): gone permanently (`지나가 버렸기 때문이다`).
