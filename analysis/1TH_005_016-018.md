# 1TH 5:16–18 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- Three brief, rhythmic present-tense imperatives:
  - **v.16**: `Rejoice at all times.`
  - **v.17**: `Pray without ceasing.`
  - **v.18**: `Give thanks in every circumstance,` + causal grounding clause (`for this is God’s will for you in Christ Jesus.`).

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `at all times` (G3842 *pantote*): always, continually (`항상`).
- `without ceasing` (G89 *adialeiptos*): unceasingly, persistently (`쉬지 말고`).
- `in every circumstance` (G1722 *en* + G3956 *pas*): in every situation/circumstance (`모든 일에`).
- `God’s will` (G2307 *thelema*): what God desires for believers (`하나님의 뜻입니다`).
