# ROM 6:23 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- Causal/explanatory `For` + parallel antithesis (`A, but B`):
  - Clause A: `the wages of sin is death,` (earned payment/result of serving sin)
  - Clause B: `but the gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord.` (unearned gracious gift located in Christ Jesus).

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `wages` (G3800 *opsonion*): soldier's pay, ration allowance, earned compensation (`죄가 주는 삯은`).
- `gift` (G5486 *charisma*): free grace-gift, contrasted directly with `wages` (`하나님께서 주시는 선물은`).
- `eternal life` (G2222 *zoe* + G166 *aionios*): everlasting life (`영원한 생명입니다`).
- `in Christ Jesus our Lord`: sphere and mediator of the gift (`우리 주 그리스도 예수 안에 있는`).
