# MAT 6:33 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- Adversative conjunction `But` (contrasting with v.32 "the Gentiles strive after all these things") + imperative verb `seek first` + compound direct object `the kingdom of God and His righteousness` + result clause `and all these things will be added unto you.`

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `seek` (G2212 *zēteō*): strive for, pursue as top priority (`먼저 구하여라 / 찾고 힘써라`).
- `kingdom of God`: God's reign/rule (`하나님의 나라`).
- `righteousness` (G1343 *dikaiosynē*): God's right way / uprightness (`그분의 의로움`).
- `be added unto you` (G4369 *prostithēmi*): be given in addition (`더해질 것이다 / 덤으로 주어질 것이다`).
