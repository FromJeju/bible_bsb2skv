# JHN 14:6 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- Emphatic self-identification (`I am the way and the truth and the life.`) with definite articles on all three nouns + exclusive negative clause (`No one comes to the Father except through Me.`).

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `way` (G3598 *hodos*): the path/road (`길`).
- `truth` (G225 *alētheia*): reality, truth (`진리`).
- `life` (G2222 *zōē*): spiritual and eternal life (`생명`).
- `except through Me` (*ei mē di' emou*): only through Me (`나를 통하지 않고서는`).
