# ROM 3:23 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- Causal conjunction `for` (continuing v.22 "There is no distinction,") + universal subject `all` + compound predicate:
  1. `have sinned` (past action with continuing effect)
  2. `and fall short of the glory of God` (present ongoing state of lacking/failing to reach God's glory).

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `all` (G3956 *pas*): every human being without distinction, both Jew and Gentile (`모든 사람이`).
- `have sinned` (G264 *hamartano*): missed the mark, committed sin (`죄를 지어`).
- `fall short of` (G5302 *hystereo*): lack, come short of, fail to reach (`이르지 못하게 되었기`).
- `the glory of God` (G1391 *doxa*): God's own glorious standard, presence, and reflection in humanity (`하나님의 영광에`).
