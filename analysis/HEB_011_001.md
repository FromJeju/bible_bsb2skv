# HEB 11:1 Structural Analysis (BSB)

## 1. Sentence Structure
- Definitional clause with transitional `Now`: `Now faith is` + two parallel predicate nominatives:
  1. `the assurance of what we hope for` (future-oriented confidence)
  2. `and the certainty of what we do not see.` (unseen-reality conviction).

## 2. Key Terms (BSB)
- `faith` (G4102 *pistis*): trusting conviction and faithfulness (`믿음은`).
- `assurance` (G5287 *hypostasis*): substance, reality, confident assurance (`확신이며`).
- `certainty` (G1650 *elegchos*): proof, conviction, inner certainty (`확증입니다`).
- `what we do not see`: unseen spiritual realities promised by God (`보이지 않는 것들에 대한`).
